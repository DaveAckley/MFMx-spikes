been written.

But that was never how it worked
on WormHole. The two streams
aren't broken up based on
distance from the beginning of
the packet. They're broken up
based on the L1 ADDRESSES that
are being written to.

So, if a packet buffer HAPPENS
to start on an even multiple of
16B, then my assumptions are
okay, on current theory.

ALIGNMENT FOR EACH PACKET
BUFFER.

I only require 4B alignment for
each packet buffer, and 16B
alignment for a whole 'endpoint'
array of packet buffers.

So that means that 20B packet
mentioned above might not be
split 16B+4B. It might be split
4B+16B say, or, 8B+12B - and in
either of those two cases, both
the 'ankle' at (12-15 from
